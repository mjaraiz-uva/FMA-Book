Comparison with existing retrosynthesis tools: Tools like ASKCOS (MIT), AiZynthFinder (AstraZeneca), and Synthia (Merck) discover synthesis routes using reaction template databases trained on millions of known reactions. Our FM-ABM discovers routes de novo from thermodynamic principles alone — no templates, no training data. This makes it uniquely suited for:

Limitations

  • Approximate kinetic barriers: An Evans-Polanyi model (Ea = E0 + α·ΔE, with E0=50 kJ/mol, α=0.3) filters weakly exothermic reactions via Boltzmann acceptance. Strongly exothermic reactions (ΔE < -167 kJ/mol) always proceed; weaker ones are probabilistically suppressed. This is not a full transition-state calculation but provides meaningful kinetic selectivity.
  • Imine preference: C=N bonds dominate over C–NH2. The biological isomer of histamine (with an imidazole ring and a primary amine) is not the thermodynamic product; it requires enzymatic biosynthesis.
  • No stereochemistry: The model does not track chirality or stereoisomers.
  • Approximate energies: BDE lookup + UFF strain is less accurate than DFT but 106× faster.
