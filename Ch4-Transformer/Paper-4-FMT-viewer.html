How to read these charts

The nanoFMT simulator models a 4-layer transformer where each layer contains a dynamic pool of competing experts (MLPs). Unlike conventional Mixture-of-Experts models, expert population is not fixed: experts are born when demand is unmet and die when idle or unfit, governed by the same 18 FMA mechanisms that drive macroeconomic firms, prebiotic molecules, and immune cells. Press Run with the Default scenario and watch the panels below tell the story of market self-organisation.

1 Training Loss

Char-level cross-entropy loss on TinyStories. Loss falls as experts specialise, but never reaches zero because the task is open-ended. In the Domain Shift scenario a spike appears when the token distribution changes abruptly at step 500, then recovers as the market spawns new experts to cover the new demand.

2 Expert Population per Layer

Number of active experts in each of the 4 transformer layers (L0–L3). The simulation starts with the initial pool (default: 4 per layer). After the warmup period, the birth/death mechanism activates: new experts are born via the Evans-Polanyi barrier (higher temperature = lower barrier = faster births), while idle experts are retired. Watch how layers grow at different rates depending on token demand distribution.

3 Birth/Death Ratio

The ratio of expert births to deaths at each market step. This is the regime indicator. Below the 1.0 threshold the market is in stagnation (more deaths than births, experts shrinking). Between 1.0 and 5.0 is the productive selection regime. Above 5.0 the market enters tar (explosive growth, like a cytokine storm in immunity or hyperinflation in economics). The High Temp scenario pushes the market toward tar; Low Temp keeps it near stagnation.

4 Unmet Demand

Tokens for which no active expert provides a strong routing gate. This is the census coverage signal: when unmet demand is high, the market tries to spawn new experts to cover the orphaned tokens. As the expert population grows, unmet demand falls. In the Domain Shift scenario, demand spikes abruptly when the distribution changes.

5 Average Fitness per Layer

Fitness = Quality × Demand. Quality is an exponential moving average of each expert's loss-reduction contribution; Demand is its share of token activations. Complexity-proportional decay gradually erodes fitness, and the depth penalty penalises deep computation chains. Experts that don't earn enough fitness are eventually retired. In the No Market ablation, fitness stays flat because no birth/death cycle exists.

6 Recipe Book

Persistent expert activation chains promoted from repeated observation — the transformer's immunological memory. When the same sequence of experts (one per layer) is activated repeatedly with low loss, it becomes a recipe. Recipes accumulate as the market discovers reliable processing pipelines. In the Domain Shift scenario, new recipes are formed as the market adapts to the shifted token distribution.

7 Dynamical Regime

A timeline strip colour-coded by regime: ■ stagnation, ■ selection, ■ tar. A healthy training run spends most of its time in the green selection regime — the same regime that corresponds to normal adaptive immunity, productive economic growth, and selection-driven prebiotic complexity.

8 Expert Grid

A visual map of expert slots in each layer. Active experts show their ID number with brightness proportional to fitness. Inactive slots show ✕. Hover over an active expert to see its fitness (F), quality (Q), and demand (D) values. Watch slots light up as new experts are born and dim as unfit ones are retired.

Scenarios to try

Default — Balanced market dynamics on TinyStories. The canonical run: watch experts grow from 4 to ~6–8 per layer, unmet demand fall, and recipes accumulate.

High Temp — Lowered Evans-Polanyi barrier. Experts spawn rapidly, pushing toward the tar regime. Population explodes but fitness stays low — many mediocre experts competing.

Low Temp — High barrier, conservative spawning. The market stays near stagnation with few births. Loss decreases slowly because the fixed expert pool can't cover token diversity.

No Market — Ablation: birth/death disabled, decay off. Expert count stays fixed at 4 per layer. Compare the loss curve against Default to see the market's contribution.

Domain Shift — At step 500, the token distribution changes (simulating a switch from stories to code). Loss spikes, unmet demand surges, and the market responds by spawning specialised experts for the new domain — exactly as an immune system mounts a secondary response to a novel pathogen.
